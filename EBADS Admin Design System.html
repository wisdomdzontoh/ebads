EBADS · Admin Panel · Web

The operator's surface — fast, dense, auditable.

The Admin Panel is the GHS-facing control surface for the EBADS engine. It builds on the mobile design system's brand and color tokens but adapts them for desktop data work — tables, filters, dialogs, charts, and dashboards designed to be scanned, sorted, and exported in seconds.

PlatformWeb · Desktop-first
StackNext.js 15 · shadcn/ui · Tailwind
AudienceAdministrators · Operators · Researchers
ModeLight & Dark

One brand, two surfaces.

The Admin Panel inherits the mobile design system's brand mark, type pairing (IBM Plex Sans + Mono), neutral ink scale, brand teal, urgency triad, and bed-availability triad. It extends them with desktop densities, a chart palette, dark mode, and form/table/dialog primitives that don't exist on mobile.

01 · Foundations

Principles

Where the mobile app optimises for one decision at a time, the panel optimises for thousands of rows at a glance.

Principle 01

Density without noise

13–14px body, 36px controls, no decorative chrome. Rows breathe through alignment and tabular numerals, not through padding inflation.

Principle 02

Every action audited

Destructive operations are confirmed; exports record actor + filters; the audit trail is always one click away. The UI never hides what the user did.

Principle 03

Server is the source of truth

No local DB, no offline cache. Connectivity loss is shown explicitly. The panel is a faithful view of the engine, never an alternate one.
